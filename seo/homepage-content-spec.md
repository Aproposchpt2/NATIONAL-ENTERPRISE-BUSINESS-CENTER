# NEBC Homepage SEO Content Specification

## Protected content

Preserve the existing imagery, hero composition, executive presentation, National Enterprise Business Center identity, and the message: An Online Full-Service Business Center.

## Primary search heading

## Online Business Readiness and Development Support

NEBC helps entrepreneurs and business owners assess readiness, identify priorities, receive a personalized action plan, and move into appropriate business-development and procurement-readiness pathways.

## Primary conversion path

1. Start the free Business Readiness Assessment.
2. Receive a personalized assessment and action plan.
3. Continue to Morgan's Office for post-assessment guidance.
4. Route into the appropriate NEBC department or procurement-intelligence platform.

## Procurement routing

- Registered federal contractors may be routed to NGCC.
- Licensed contractors seeking supported state and local opportunities may be routed to NAT-CORP.
- Routing must reflect eligibility and service scope; it must not imply guaranteed contract access, qualification, or award.
