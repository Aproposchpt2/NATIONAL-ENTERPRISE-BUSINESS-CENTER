Business Readiness Assessment for Nevada Businesses

A readiness assessment is not a pass/fail score. It is a structured way to identify what is working, what is missing, and which business-development actions should come next.

Start the NEBC assessment

Four readiness dimensions

Business readiness

Business model, market, customers, licensing, operations and the clarity of the opportunity being pursued.

Financial readiness

Revenue history, cash flow, bookkeeping, forecasts, capital need and the evidence supporting assumptions.

Planning readiness

Goals, milestones, use of funds, implementation plan and the ability to explain how growth will be executed.

Management readiness

Leadership capacity, staffing, controls, responsibilities and the ability to manage the next stage of growth.

What NEBC does after assessment

NEBC converts intake responses into a personalized assessment report and then moves the user into post-assessment guidance. Morgan's role is advisory: diagnose gaps, explain options and help sequence next steps without promising financing or guaranteed outcomes.

A readiness assessment does not replace legal, accounting, lending or licensing advice. Use Nevada's official agencies and qualified professionals for controlling requirements.

Complementary Nevada support

Nevada GOED identifies Nevada SBDC, SCORE, Women's Business Center, MBDA and other partners as sources of planning, mentorship and capital-access support. Review Nevada GOED business support resources.
