Business Planning for Nevada Businesses

A useful business plan is a decision tool. It should connect market demand, operating capacity, financial assumptions, milestones and risks to the next stage of the business.

Start the NEBC assessment

Core planning questions

Who is the customer?

Define the buyer, problem, value proposition, geography and evidence that demand exists.

How will the business deliver?

Document suppliers, staffing, systems, facilities, licenses, partners and operational constraints.

What does the model require financially?

Connect pricing, revenue assumptions, expenses, margins, cash needs and growth milestones.

What could derail the plan?

Identify market, customer, financing, staffing, regulatory and execution risks with practical mitigations.

Planning and advising should work together

NEBC's assessment can surface gaps before a business invests significant time in a formal plan. Post-assessment guidance can then help prioritize the sections and evidence that need the most work.
