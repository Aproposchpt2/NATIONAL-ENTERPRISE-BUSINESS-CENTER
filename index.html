Procurement-Readiness Pathway

From Business Readiness to Government Opportunity

When procurement becomes the appropriate next step, NEBC helps direct the business toward the correct APROPOS opportunity platform.

Registered federal contractors may continue into the National Government Contract Center. Other licensed contractors seeking applicable state and local opportunities may continue into the National Corporate Contract Exchange.

NEBC remains the business-development and readiness center. NGCC and NAT-CORP are separate downstream procurement services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the National Enterprise Business Center?

NEBC is an online full-service business center operated by APROPOS Group LLC. It provides an assessment-led pathway into personalized business guidance, development support, operational readiness, and procurement readiness when appropriate.

Where does the NEBC process begin?

The process begins with the Business Readiness Assessment. Your responses support a personalized business action plan and determine the most appropriate next step.

What happens in Morgan’s Office?

Morgan’s Office is the post-assessment advisory step. Morgan helps interpret the pathway, identifies the primary recommendation, and routes the business to the appropriate department, resource, or service.

Does NEBC provide government contracts directly?

No. NEBC prepares and routes businesses when procurement is appropriate. NGCC and NAT-CORP are separate APROPOS procurement services for downstream government-opportunity access.

How much does membership cost?

NEBC includes a 14-day free trial and then costs $39 per month as a recurring subscription. You may cancel before the next billing date, and paid access remains active through the current billing period.

Does NEBC replace an attorney, accountant, tax professional, or financial advisor?

No. NEBC provides business-development guidance and readiness support. Legal, tax, accounting, financial, and other licensed-professional matters require the appropriate qualified professional.
