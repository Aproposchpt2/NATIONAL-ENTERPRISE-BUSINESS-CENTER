Frequently Asked Questions

What is NEBC?

NEBC is an online full-service business center offering assessments, personalized action planning, guidance, and routing into relevant business-support services.

Where does the process begin?

The process begins with the Business Readiness Assessment.

Does NEBC replace professional legal, tax, or financial advice?

No. NEBC provides business-development guidance and appropriate referrals; specialized professional advice should be obtained when needed.

How does NEBC connect to procurement services?

Businesses may be routed toward NGCC or NAT-CORP when contractor status, readiness, and goals indicate an appropriate procurement pathway.
