Business Funding Opportunity Center

Build on your NEBC business profile to identify funding paths that fit your business. The first controlled funding channels are SBA Microloan intermediaries and SBA-licensed Small Business Investment Companies (SBICs).

SBA Microloan

NEBC compares your funding profile with controlled SBA Microloan intermediary records. The system surfaces source records for further review without representing that a lender has approved or prequalified the business.

SBIC Investment Capital

NEBC can also compare growth-capital needs with controlled SBA SBIC records. A match indicates relevance for review, not investment eligibility or commitment.
