Before Contacting a Source

  1. Review the provider's current service area and program requirements.
  2. Confirm the requested amount and use of proceeds fit the program.
  3. Prepare the documents the provider requests.
  4. Ask about underwriting/investment criteria, timing, fees and terms.
  5. Apply or proceed only through the provider's authoritative channel.
