Business Funding Opportunity Center

Build on your NEBC business profile to identify funding paths that fit your business. The first controlled funding channels are SBA Microloan intermediaries and SBA-licensed Small Business Investment Companies (SBICs).

SBA Microloan

Designed for businesses seeking smaller debt-financing amounts for uses such as working capital, inventory, supplies, machinery, fixtures, and equipment. NEBC will use controlled SBA-source records to identify relevant intermediary paths.

SBIC Investment Capital

For businesses whose capital needs and growth profile may warrant investment-oriented financing. NEBC will use controlled SBA SBIC records as the initial investment-capital source universe.

Build Your Funding Profile
